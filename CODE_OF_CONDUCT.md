# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
Welcome contributors with different languages, backgrounds and levels of experience.
Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, publish private information or pressure anyone
to disclose confidential material.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation. They will
consider context, explain decisions where safe, and avoid disclosing reports without
consent. Request a private contact from a maintainer without posting sensitive details.
Good-faith concerns about a decision may be raised with another maintainer.
